# How to Register

To create content, follow people and Limited Editions, and interact with other users, you must first register.

You can register for Nuance using either:

* [Internet Identity](https://identity.ic0.app/)
* [Google NFID](https://nfid.one/)
* [Stoic Wallet](https://www.stoicwallet.com/)
* [Bitfinity Wallet](https://wallet.bitfinity.network/)

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Ekran görüntüsü 2024-04-24 200140.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Home Page</p></figcaption></figure>

**Step 1:** On the Nuance home page (above) select either the **Internet Identity (II)**, **NFID**, **Stoic Wallet** or **Bitfinity Wallet** register buttons on the left hand side of the screen. Once the flow is completed, you will be directed to the "registration" page (below). To access Stoic and Bitfinity Wallet options, you can click "Other options".

**Step 2:** Create your @handle (which _cannot_ be changed), and your display name (which _can_ be changed). You can also add an avatar.

**Step 3:** Accept T\&Cs and select **Next**.

**Step 4:** Start blogging, reading, following, commenting, and applauding.

**NB:** We have a known issue with Stoic Wallet registration which requires clearing cookies to register successfully.

**NB:** If using Brave you will need to ensure you enable cookies (shields down).
