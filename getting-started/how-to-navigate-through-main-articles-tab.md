# How to Navigate Through Main Articles Tab

You will see the blog feed just below the search bar. This is the area where published articles are shown to you. You can use the tabs between the feed and search bar to change the feed options.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/feed tab.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Articles Feed Tab</p></figcaption></figure>

The dropdown menu on the right will help you filter the time range. This menu is only active in the Popular tab. How to access your personal articles is explained later in the wiki.

* The **Writers** tab lists articles from authors you follow. This is the most convenient option to follow the content of your favorite authors.&#x20;
* The **Topics** tab allows you to easily access articles published on the topics you follow.
* The **New** tab allows you to access recently published articles by sorting them from newest to oldest.
* The **Popular** tab allows you to review the most [applauded ](../tipping/)and trending articles. When you click on the Popular tab, the dropdown menu will be active on the right side of the screen, allowing you to filter the time range. In this way, you can sort popular articles daily, weekly, monthly or all time.
