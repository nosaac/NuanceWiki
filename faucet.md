# Faucet

Support content creators and contribute to the community with Faucet, all without worrying about your wallet.

The Faucet system allows you to claim 50 NUA Tokens once a week. Tokens claimed with Faucet are accepted as "Restricted NUA". You can only [tip ](tipping/)other writers or [susbscribe ](subscription/)their content with your restricted tokens.

The system notifies users with a notification when there are tokens available for claim. To claim your free Nua, you need to go to your wallet page by clicking on the **Request Free Nua** button in the notification or by clicking on **Profile Icon > Wallet**.

<figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/nuanotification (1).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Claim Notification</p></figcaption></figure>

You can make your refill request by using the **Request Free NUA** button on your wallet page.

<figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/nuawallet (1).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Request Free NUA</p></figcaption></figure>

You are allowed to request free NUA up until a total of 50 Free NUA in your wallet. These Free NUA tokens first need to be ‘spent’ on the Nuance platform like applauding or subscribing before you can trade them. They will be added to your wallet immediately.

_Disclaimer: Please be aware that the usage of the faucet system is subject to legal regulations regarding cryptocurrencies and digital assets in your respective jurisdiction. It is your responsibility to understand and comply with local laws before engaging with the faucet system to claim or use tokens._
