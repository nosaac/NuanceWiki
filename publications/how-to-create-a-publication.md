# How to Create a Publication

First you need to request a publication to be created. Do this by asking us on our [OpenChat](https://oc.app/community/3qzyb-ryaaa-aaaar-ateiq-cai/?ref=ya523-5iaaa-aaaaf-aexra-cai). You will need to provide:

1. Your preferred Publication handle (OneWord)
2. Your preferred Publication title
3. A Nuance handle for an existing user (to be the editor (admin) of the publication)

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-02-12 at 12.41.53.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Your Publications</p></figcaption></figure>

After your Publication is created, you can start customizing your Publication by clicking the **Edit Publication** button. Additionally, you can also specify a subtitle for your publication on this page. All customizations you can make on your publication are covered in the next section.
