# Tipping

With the Applaud feature, you can tip and support the authors you like - just like on Medium. With Nuance, applause is not free (you have to "[put your money where your mouth is](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/put-money-where-mouth-is)"). When you applaud an article you transfer tokens from your wallet to the writer's wallet. Let's explore.

_Disclaimer: Please be aware that the usage of the tipping system is subject to legal regulations regarding cryptocurrencies and digital assets in your respective jurisdiction. It is your responsibility to understand and comply with local laws before engaging with the tipping system and claim or use tokens._
