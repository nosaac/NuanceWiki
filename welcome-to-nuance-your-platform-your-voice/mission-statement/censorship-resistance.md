# Censorship Resistance

Content moderation in legacy platforms is often a "black box." Private entities can suppress or remove articles without a clear explanation, often stifling diverse perspectives or sensitive discussions.

True freedom of expression is impossible if a central authority can manipulate it. A global exchange of ideas requires a space where content cannot be arbitrarily tampered with.

Building on the Internet Computer Protocol (ICP) provides tamper-proof security. Content stays accessible because it lives on-chain, protected from centralized interference by the decentralized nature of the network.
