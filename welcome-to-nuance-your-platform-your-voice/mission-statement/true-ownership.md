# True Ownership

In the current blogging landscape, writers often discover that owning their content is merely a legal theory. If a platform decides to delete an account or change its database, the writer loses everything. There is no technical proof of who wrote what first.<a class="button secondary">+2</a>

Without concrete proof of originality, intellectual property rights become difficult to enforce. Credibility is tied to a platform's existence rather than the work itself. Creators deserve technical sovereignty that matches their legal rights.

Nuance provides on-chain evidence of every post. The link between the creator and the article is hard-coded into the blockchain. This gives writers a verifiable asset that exists independently of any single company's database.
