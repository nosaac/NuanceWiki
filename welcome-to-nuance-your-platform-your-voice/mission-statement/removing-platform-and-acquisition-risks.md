# Removing Platform and Acquisition Risks

A common fear for digital creators is the "exit." A platform they trust gets acquired by a larger corporation with a different agenda. Suddenly, terms of service change, features disappear, or the platform is shut down entirely to reduce competition.

Long-form publishing requires a permanent home. Creators cannot thrive if the foundation of their work can be sold or altered to suit a new owner's profit margins.

As a community-owned protocol on the Internet Computer, Nuance cannot be sold to a centralized entity. The [DAO](../dao.md) structure protects the infrastructure as a public good, keeping the mission stable and the environment secure for the long term
